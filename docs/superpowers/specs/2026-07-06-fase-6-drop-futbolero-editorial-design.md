# Fase 6 Drop Futbolero Editorial Design

## Goal

Make Mundo JJersey feel less generic and less AI-generated by turning the current navy/gold catalog into a more specific football-streetwear drop experience.

## Direction

The selected direction is **Drop futbolero editorial**: a curated football/streetwear catalog with the rhythm of a drop, the clarity of a catalog, and visual details inspired by garment tags, match sheets, merch labels, and product lookbooks.

## What Must Change

- The hero must become a composed visual scene, not only large text and a decorative circle.
- The page must stop repeating the same eyebrow/title/copy/grid grammar in every section.
- Category navigation must stop looking like three equal generic cards.
- Product cards must feel framed and collectible, not like standard ecommerce cards.
- Product detail must feel like a selling sheet: image, fit/stock facts, CTA, and confidence copy.
- Motion must remain CSS-only, accessible, and based on `transform`/`opacity`.
- The implementation must keep Astro static, Sanity data flow, WhatsApp CTA, routes, and tests intact.

## Visual Rules

- Keep navy as the main brand surface and gold as an accent, not a dominant field.
- Add subtle fixed grain/noise and technical linework to reduce flat digital surfaces.
- Use a stronger display stack for headings and labels, favoring condensed sports/street typography.
- Avoid repeated uppercase eyebrows; use mixed labels, tags, stamps, and structured product facts.
- Avoid generic icon cards and equal 3-column feature rows.
- Keep WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, touch-friendly mobile, and `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Target Surfaces

- Home: hero, latest products, categories, about, trust, FAQ.
- Catalog: all-products intro, category links, product grid, empty state.
- Category pages: category-specific hero and context.
- Product detail: gallery area, facts, variants, WhatsApp CTA, related products.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `npm test` passes with a stronger visual contract.
- `npm run check` has 0 errors/warnings/hints.
- `npm run build:web` succeeds and generates category routes.
- Home contains a distinct drop-scene composition.
- Categories use asymmetric hierarchy, with camisetas as the dominant block.
- Cards include label-strip/ticket details and variant-aware stock.
- Product detail uses `/producto/[slug]` and has stronger product-sheet hierarchy.
- Reduced motion remains supported.
